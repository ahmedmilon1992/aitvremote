The short version.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the data controller is Shahadat, contactable at ahmedmilon1992@gmail.com. If you are located in the EU/UK and require a postal address for a formal request, please email us and we will provide it.

2. Information the App handles

a) Information stored only on your device. Your saved TVs, their names and room labels, your favorites, and your app settings are stored locally on your device (and included in your own iCloud device backup if you have that enabled). We cannot access this information.

b) Local network information. To find and control your TVs, the App communicates with them over your home Wi-Fi using device names, IP addresses, and each brand's control protocol. This stays on your local network and is not transmitted to us.

c) Microphone and speech (voice control). If you use voice control, your speech is processed by Apple's on-device speech recognition to turn it into TV commands. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to us or any third party.

d) Live Help requests. If you choose to use "Get Live Help," you provide your name, phone number, email address, a description of your TV, and your message. This information is transmitted by email to the developer through our email-delivery processor (Web3Forms) solely so we can respond to your support request. It is not used for marketing.

e) Remote Share. If you choose to use Remote Share, remote-control button presses are relayed between two devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). This carries control commands and the TV's display name, not personal content, and the session ends when you close it.

f) Purchases. Subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information; we do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive from Apple only the information needed to confirm your subscription status.

g) Correspondence. If you email us or use "Report a Problem," we receive your email address and the content of your message, and use it only to respond to you.

3. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect usage analytics, behavioral profiles, advertising identifiers (IDFA), precise or coarse location data, contacts, or browsing history. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

4. How we use information and our legal bases

We use the limited information above to: (i) provide the App's features and control your TVs; (ii) provide support when you request it; and (iii) process purchases through Apple. Where GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (responding to inquiries and keeping the App working and secure), and consent (for microphone access, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies. We rely on the following service providers strictly to provide the App: Apple Inc. (iCloud and the App Store — see apple.com/legal/privacy) and Web3Forms (delivering Live Help requests to us by email — see web3forms.com/privacy-policy). We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal rights, to prevent fraud or abuse, or in connection with a business transfer.

6. International data transfers

Our processors may store and process information on servers in the United States or other countries. Where information is transferred out of the EU/UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Data retention

Information stored on your device remains until you remove a TV or delete the App. Live Help messages and support correspondence are retained only as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period thereafter, after which they are deleted. Remote Share session data is transient and removed when the session ends.
