Common questions

My TV won't connect. Make sure your iPhone and your TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, that the TV is turned on, then open the app and rescan. The in-app "How it works" guide has step-by-step setup for each TV brand.

The app can't turn my TV on. Power-on over Wi-Fi only works while your iPhone is on the same home network, and your TV must keep its network awake in standby. See the per-brand "network standby / power on with mobile" steps in the app's setup guide. A wired (Ethernet) connection is the most reliable.

Which TVs are supported? Roku, Samsung, and LG are fully supported today, with more brands being added.
